# Credits

Third-party material shipped in the mod jar. The per-file list (path, source, author, licence, URL) lives in
[`src/main/resources/assets/dndsheets/CREDITS.txt`](src/main/resources/assets/dndsheets/CREDITS.txt) and is checked
by the build (`checkAssetCredits`).

  Re-rasterized and tinted by `tools/import_game_icons.py`.
- **Font** (`font/cinzel.ttf`, screen titles): Cinzel, (c) The Cinzel Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1
  (`assets/dndsheets/font/ofl-cinzel.txt`).

- **Rules content**: SRD 5.1, CC BY 4.0 (see `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`).
- **Creature models** (`geo/entity`, `animations/entity`, `textures/entity`): the archetypes with a `mask.png` are
  original work, generated by `tools/blockbench/mobs/*.py`.
- **Imported creature models**: `wraith`, `specter`, `necromancer`, `crone`, `warlock`, `brood_mother`, `web_spider`,

- **Import policy:** permissive licences only (CC0, CC BY, MIT, Apache, Unlicense), and the licence has to cover
  the art itself (the repo's LICENSE, with no carve-out for assets). Never CC BY-SA, NC, LGPL/GPL/MPL or
  all-rights-reserved. Never a re-upload of a Mojang mob (whatever its stated licence) or Mojang's textures. A model
  that shares the vanilla humanoid/skeleton box layout is judged one by one on what its author added and whose
  texture it is; the decision is recorded in `tools/blockbench/import/javamodel.py`, and Mojang's own boxes are
  never copied. How candidates are found and ranked is in `reference/creature-model-survey.md`.
